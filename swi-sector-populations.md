# SW Interworlds sector population and workforce growth

Derived 2026-10-01/06 from SW Interworlds 0.9.1 HF (`version="091"`, 2025-12-24), galaxy `swi_galaxy_macro` (22 of 32 gamestarts; the 9 showcase gamestarts use `sws_galaxy_macro` and are excluded).

## Engine population: 0 everywhere

SWI defines no `<system>`/`<planets>` on any cluster and no `<worlds>` on any sector (0 of 220), and `maxpopulation` appears in no SWI file. By the same derivation as `vanilla-sector-populations.md`, `GetSectorPopulation` is 0 and the workforce growth bonus is +0.0% in every SWI sector. SWI's `parameters.xml` does not patch the vanilla `<workforce>` block. Not measured in a running save.

## Estimated lore population

Because the engine sees no population, the table below estimates one per sector. Selection rules:

1. **Wookieepedia Canon** infobox population if the canon article has a numeric value, else **Wookieepedia Legends**. An article is Legends if its title ends in `/Legends` or its `{{Top}}` template carries `leg` or `canon=`.
2. Among several values, the one whose era is **closest to 4 ABY**, SWI's setting (its faction texts are written "as of 4 ABY"). Era labels map to year ranges (e.g. Imperial Era 19 BBY-4 ABY, New Republic Era 5-28 ABY, "modern" 25-45 ABY). An unlabelled value is preferred over a dated one more than 10 years from 4 ABY. Four values were set by hand (Alderaan, Ithor, Mon Calamari, Celanon) and say why in the basis column.
3. Ranges use the geometric mean ("1 to 10 billion" -> 3.16 billion); "less than X" uses X. Many Legends figures are *Essential Atlas* values for c. 25 ABY, the only ones published.
4. No usable Wookieepedia figure: a decade tier (0, 1,000 ... 10 billion) read from SWI's own sector description (`t/0001.xml`, page 999840).
5. **SWI lore override:** where a plausibility check (2026-10-06) found SWI's own 4 ABY description contradicting the Wookieepedia value, SWI wins, since its galaxy is the one being played. The basis column quotes SWI and keeps the Wookieepedia value.

Sources: 36 canon, 96 Legends, 82 SWI description tiers, 1 placeholder with no source data, 5 SWI lore overrides. 101 of 220 sectors reach CE's 100 million hub threshold.

**These are estimates, not engine values.** Description tiers are judgement calls. Wookieepedia matches were made by name; four wrong matches were removed (Kira: a character; Kuna: a disambiguation page; Roqoo: a star; Greater Javin: a region) and seven names were mapped to their planet article (Brentaal -> Brentaal IV, Mon Calamari -> Mon Cala/Dac, Tirahn -> Tirahnn, Telos -> Telos IV, Garos -> Garos IV, Rorak -> Rorak 4, Roche -> Roche asteroid field). Only the namesake world is counted, not other worlds in the system.

Wookieepedia content is CC BY-SA 3.0. Population figures are facts, but credit Wookieepedia if this data ships.

The "Bonus" column is the vanilla workforce formula (`parameters.xml`: +1% per 10 million, capped at +1,000%) applied to the estimate. In the Wookieepedia column the bold link is the one the value came from; the others were checked.

Economy is NOT a population signal: SWI sets economy 0 on populous worlds such as Kuat, Baros, Rothana and Renatasia. Economy, security and sunlight are the mapdefaults `<area>` values, inherited from the cluster dataset in every SWI sector. `factionlogic=false` disallows NPC faction logic in that cluster; in vanilla scripts this stops factions picking an ownerless sector as an invasion/claim target (`factiongoal_invade_space.xml`); any engine-side effect is unverified.
